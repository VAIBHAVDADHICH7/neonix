Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of rooftop solar in Jaipur? +

The cost depends on system size, your daily usage, and specific site requirements. Contact us today via our form above to receive a completely free, customized quote tailored to your home or business.

Is government subsidy available? +

Yes, under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, subsidies of up to ₹78,000 are available for residential solar systems. As your dedicated solar installation partner, we handle the entire National Portal registration and subsidy claim process for you.

How much can I save with solar? +

Depending on your system size and consumption, you can save up to 80–90% on electricity bills. With an optimally sized system and net metering, many of our clients achieve absolute zero electricity cost over time.

Do you provide maintenance services? +

Absolutely. We offer complete Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and dedicated after-sales support to ensure your solar investment performs at peak efficiency. We're here for you whenever you need us.
